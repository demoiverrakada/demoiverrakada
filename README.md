# Umang Agarwal

Software Development Engineer at Amazon and independent AI safety researcher.

I study whether model-auditing and model-diffing methods can recover the
conditional behaviour introduced through fine-tuning—not merely identify the
training topic.

My work uses controlled fine-tunes, blinded evaluations, causal activation
interventions, preregistration, adversarial controls, and reproducible
evaluation pipelines.

## Current research

### [Finetune Trace: Behavior or Topic?](https://github.com/demoiverrakada/finetune-trace-behavior)

Testing whether model-diffing methods recover a fine-tuned model's actual
conditional behaviour. Includes eight controlled Qwen3-1.7B fine-tunes, a
720-response blinded benchmark, causal activation interventions, matched
controls, and published negative results.
